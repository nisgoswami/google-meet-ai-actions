Turn the discussion into a ticket.

Connect once. Send and Review buttons appear on messages in your Meet Gemini panel.

Configured on this browser profile.

Sign in with Linear

Approve access in Linear, then choose the team for your tickets.

Bring your own API key

Use Read and Create issues, restricted to your team, for the searchable update picker. Create issues includes updating issues. No general Write, Comments, or Admin permissions are needed. Read is optional if you only create tickets or manually replace descriptions. Open Linear settings ↗

In Linear, open your team, press Ctrl/Cmd+K, choose Copy model UUID, and select the Team entry. Entering this once avoids granting workspace Read access.

New ticket defaults

Choose a backlog/status and assignee. You can override both in Send to Linear. Existing tickets keep their status and assignee unless you explicitly change them.

Gemini prompt templates

Choose a topic when preparing a ticket. Templates stay in this browser and can be edited without reconnecting Linear.

Available values: {{topic}}, {{issueId}}, {{issueTitle}}, {{issueDescription}}, {{teamPrefix}}. Update prompts receive the selected ticket's current content.


In your next call

  1. Click Action → Tickets → Create and enter the topic discussed in the call.
  2. Click Send to Linear on that message, choose an action, and confirm.
  3. Use Review to edit it, or use Action → Tickets → Update to search recent tickets and prepare an update from the selected ticket.

Your login tokens or API key stay in this extension’s local browser storage. Ticket searches go directly to Linear. A selected ticket is copied into Ask Gemini only when you choose it. No audio, captions, or meeting history are recorded. Credentials are not synced or exposed to Meet.
