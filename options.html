THREADPORT · GOOGLE MEET → LINEAR

Turn the discussion into a ticket.

Connect once. Send and Review buttons appear on messages in your Meet Gemini panel.

Configured on this browser profile.

Use Create issues only, restricted to your team. No Read, Write, Comments, or Admin permissions are needed. Linear calls this “Create and update issues.” Open Linear settings ↗

In Linear, open your team, press Ctrl/Cmd+K, choose Copy model UUID, and select the Team entry. Entering this once avoids granting workspace Read access.

In your next call

  1. Ask Gemini to draft a ticket with Title: and Description:.
  2. Click Send to Linear on that message to create one issue.
  3. Use Review to edit it, or choose Update an existing issue and enter its ID.

Your key stays in this extension’s local browser storage. Only the ticket fields you send and the Meet link go to Linear. No audio, captions, or meeting history are recorded. Your key is not synced or exposed to Meet.
